Privacy Policy

Extra Sugar (سكر زيادة) · Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Extra Sugar (package name com.alaafahmy.extrasugar), developed by Alaa Fahmy, handles information.

Short version

Extra Sugar does not collect, share, or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your own device: unlocked levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives, daily gift status, and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration). This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you clear the game's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

The game uses only the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback while you play. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings.

Internet, ads, and third parties

The game does not connect to the internet, does not show ads, does not include analytics, and does not use any third-party service that collects data.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that change how information is handled (for example, ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the update date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: alaafahmy297@gmail.com
